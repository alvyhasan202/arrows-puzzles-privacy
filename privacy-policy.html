Arrows Puzzles - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 (ads section updated)

This policy explains what information the game Arrows Puzzles ("the app", "we") uses and how it is handled. We keep it short because the app collects very little.

1. Information we do not collect

The app has no accounts and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (current level, solved daily puzzles) and your settings (sound, vibration, ad preferences) are saved only on your device. We do not receive this data. Removing the app deletes it.

3. Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. This is handled by those companies under their own policies:

Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from apps that use its services

Ads may be personalized using your device's advertising ID. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization at any time in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads). Where the law requires consent for personalized ads (for example in Europe), the app requests non-personalized ads only.

4. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. The only third party that receives data through the app is the advertising provider described above.

6. Security

Because the app keeps its data on your device and does not run accounts or servers for players, there is no player database for us to protect or leak.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date above updated.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, email us at alvyhasan2021@gmail.com.
